About WiiVolve

WiiVolve was a Wii news site from 2006 to 2008. It’s back as a single page that gathers Wii news and homebrew scene activity from around the web every hour and links you to the source.

How it works: every hour a small script reads the sources below, keeps what’s about the original Wii, removes duplicates and rebuilds this page. Nobody edits the list by hand. Links to stories found through Google News go through Google first.
